What the chain learns

An observer can see

  • The commitments on the allowlist, and its Merkle root
  • That someone on the list was admitted, and when
  • One nullifier per admission, and the running tally
  • That the sponsor paid the fee, and how much DUST

An observer cannot see

  • Your secret — it never leaves this browser
  • Which entry on the list is yours
  • Which admission was your admission
  • Whether two admissions came from related people
